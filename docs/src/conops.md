# Concept of Operations

CONOPs (Concept of Operations) is how a designer plans to use a spacecraft once it is in orbit. This involves how the vehicle moves, what actions it takes autonomously, and how operators interact with it.

The _raison d'etre_ for a given mission is captured in its CONOPs. The _raison d'etre_ of OpenSOAP is to simulate and iterate on CONOPs.

## A scenario

You may have a satellite going around the Earth. It does this for a reason—probably somebody wants (or somebodies want) more data. What kind of data?

- Maybe images of the Earth
- Maybe communications with other satellites
- Maybe communications with the ground
- Maybe images or spectra of stars or planets

Regardless, the thing wants to point somewhere to get data. And it may want to send it to the ground. Oh, yeah, and it needs power.
